## 1. bagby-hot-springs — Bagby Hot Springs, Mt. Hood NF, OR

**Verdict: RESOLVED — CLOSED. Do not publish as open/visitable.**

- Dataset flag: "the site has had extended closures in recent years" — current status had to be confirmed with the Forest Service.
- Current authoritative status: **closed and inaccessible.**
- Forest Service source: Mt. Hood National Forest, Austin Fire Closure, **Forest Order No. 06-06-05-26-19**, effective **October 16, 2026 through December 31, 2026, or until updated or rescinded**, prohibiting going into or being upon National Forest System lands, roads, or trails within the fire closure area.
  - URL: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r06/mthood/alerts/clackamas-river-rd-fires
  - Paraphrase: the order (executed October 16, 2026) closes the Clackamas River Ranger District fire area to all public entry.
- Corroborating reporting of the Forest Service position after the closure area was reduced: "Forest Roads 63 and 70 remain closed, and Bagby Hot Springs remains inaccessible and closed."
  - URL: https://www.kxl.com/mt-hood-national-forest-reduces-closure-area-as-austin-narrows-fires-see-increased-containment/
- Further corroboration, September 29, 2026: "Bagby Hot Springs is closed and inaccessible due to ongoing cleanup operations related to the wildfires… unreachable because of ongoing Austin Fire orders," quoting USFS–Mt. Hood National Forest.
  - URL: https://theoutlookonline.com/2026/09/29/bagby-hot-springs-is-closed-despite-what-your-maps-app-says/
- **Publish implication:** any listing must state CLOSED (Austin Fire order, through December 31, 2026 unless rescinded), not merely "check conditions." Third-party maps/apps showing it open are explicitly wrong per the Forest Service.

## 2. granite-hot-springs-pool — Granite Hot Springs Pool, Bridger-Teton NF, WY

**Verdict: STILL_HELD — the Forest Service page is internally conflicting / stale; current status could not be confirmed.**

- Dataset flag: at wave-3 review the Forest Service page "noted the pool closed with a planned summer reopening."
- What the Forest Service page says today (accessed 2026-10-25): the same page still carries the banner **"Site Closed — Granite Hot Springs is closed due to deteriorating access conditions. Granite Creek Road is scheduled to open to wheeled vehicles on May 1, and Granite Hot Springs is planned to reopen for the summer season on May 22"** — with no year and no dated update — while the body of the *same page* states **"Summer season usually opens late May and ends October 31st"** (hours 10 a.m.–8 p.m., 10 a.m.–6 p.m. after Labor Day) and a winter season from early December.
  - URL: https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/bridger-teton/recreation/granite-hot-springs-pool
- Why the flag stands: on October 25 the page simultaneously implies (a) closed awaiting a May reopening, and (b) in summer season until October 31. Those cannot both be current. No dated 2026 Forest Service reopening notice, closure notice, or alert resolving the contradiction was found. Secondary sources (e.g., Visit Sublette County) repeat the same undated seasonal schedule (late May–October 31) but are not the Forest Service and do not resolve the banner.
- **Publish implication:** HOLD. Do not publish a current open/closed status for Granite until the Forest Service page is updated or the Jackson Ranger District / pool phone (1-307-690-6323, as listed on the FS page) confirms the fall 2026 status.

## 3. fairmont-hot-springs-resort — Fairmont Hot Springs Resort, Anaconda, MT

**Verdict: RESOLVED — CORRECTED. There is NO public day-use pool access; pools are guests/members only.**

- Dataset flag: "recent secondary reporting says pool access may be limited to registered guests and members."
- Operator (authoritative) source, accessed 2026-10-25 — the resort's own Hot Springs page states verbatim: **"Both indoor and outdoor pools are open only to registered overnight guests staying at the resort or condominium association and pool and golf members. Guests staying at the Fairmont RV Park and Chalets may use the pool for a daily fee, with proper credentials. Pools are open from 7am-11pm daily."** The same page links a "Why aren't the pools open for day use?" explainer.
  - URL: https://fairmontmontana.com/hot-springs/
- **Publish implication:** the secondary reporting was right. Any listing must say pools are **not** open to the general public for day use — registered overnight guests and pool/golf members only, plus RV Park/Chalet guests for a daily fee. Do not list Fairmont as a public/day-use soak.

## 4. saratoga-hobo-hot-springs — Saratoga Hobo Hot Springs (Hobo Pool), Saratoga, WY

**Verdict: STILL_HELD — partially corroborated, but no Town of Saratoga source confirms the ownership / free / 24-hour claims as flagged.**

- Dataset flag: town-ownership, free, and 24-hour claims came from secondary sources; the dataset's only source was the Saratoga-Platte Valley Chamber of Commerce, and the claims "must be re-confirmed with the Town of Saratoga."
- Town of Saratoga (authoritative) source found — the Town's own Hobo Pool page, accessed 2026-10-25:
  - URL: https://www.townofsaratoga.org/205/Hobo-Pool
  - What it says: **"The Hobo Pool is open"**; it describes the historic hot pool on the banks of the Platte River, lists restrictions (no alcohol/open containers, no smoking, no glass), and gives the Town of Saratoga address (P.O. Box 486, 110 E. Spring Avenue, Saratoga, WY 82331) and phone (307) 326-8335.
  - What it does **not** say: the Town page is **silent on fee (free or otherwise), silent on hours (24-hour or otherwise), and contains no explicit statement that the Town owns/operates the pool.** Hosting the page on the Town site is suggestive, but it is not the confirmation the flag asked for.
- Other authoritative-adjacent corroboration (not the Town): Carbon County Visitors Council / Discover Carbon County states "Free and open to the public 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, the Saratoga Hot Pool…" — this confirms fee + hours from a county tourism authority, but says nothing about town ownership.
  - URL: https://www.discovercarboncounty.com/index.php/places-to-visit/saratoga
- Why the flag stands: the full claim set (town-owned **and** free **and** 24 hours) still rests on the Chamber page — the dataset's original, non-Town source. The Town's own page confirms only that the pool exists and is open.
- **Publish implication:** HOLD the "town-owned, free, open 24 hours" wording as a single confirmed claim until the Town states it (or a Town ordinance/budget/facilities page is found). Separately sourced facts that *can* be cited today: Town page = pool is open; Carbon County Visitors Council = free and open to the public 24/7.

## 5. fifth-water-hot-springs — Fifth Water Hot Springs, Uinta-Wasatch-Cache NF, UT

**Verdict: RESOLVED — the Utah DEQ harmful-bacteria advisory is CURRENT and confirmed; the Forest Service page shows no closure.**

- Dataset flag: "the Utah DEQ recreational-monitoring advisory for harmful bacteria referenced on trail listings — current water advisories, closures and road conditions must be confirmed."
- Utah DEQ (authoritative) source, accessed 2026-10-25 — Fifth Water Hot Springs Recreational Monitoring page (page last updated 2026-08-27), most recent update **July 15, 2026: "BE CAREFUL: Harmful bacteria may be present at Fifth Water Hot Springs. Multiple people have developed severe rashes and blisters after recreating there this summer."** DEQ recommends drying off thoroughly, not hiking out in wet clothes, showering immediately after soaking, not dunking your head, not drinking the water or letting pets drink it, and avoiding algae on pool sides/bottoms. An earlier May 5, 2026 update carried the same warning. No rescission or "OK to recreate" update supersedes the July 15 warning on the page.
  - URL: https://deq.utah.gov/dwq/water-quality-health/fifth-water-hot-springs
- Forest Service status: the Uinta-Wasatch-Cache NF Fifth Water Trail #015 page carries **no closure banner and no advisory**; it describes the trail from Three Forks / Diamond Fork normally and states only "This trail is managed by the Forest Service" (Spanish Fork Ranger District, 801-798-3571). The Forest Service page is silent on the DEQ advisory — silence there does not cancel DEQ's warning.
  - URL: https://www.fs.usda.gov/recarea/uwcnf/recarea/?recid=9861
- **Publish implication:** publishable only **with** the DEQ warning carried prominently (harmful bacteria may be present; severe rashes/blisters reported summer 2026; follow DEQ precautions). Do not publish as an unqualified soak, and do not describe the trail as "closed" — DEQ advises caution, and the Forest Service lists no closure.
