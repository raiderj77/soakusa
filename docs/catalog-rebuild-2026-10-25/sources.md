Cadences are editorial re-review targets, not claims of continuous monitoring. If a source is stale, unavailable or outside its authority, fail closed. Secondary/background sources cannot establish current permission, closures, fees, hazards or emergency information. Current statements from responsible authorities must be reviewed in context and attributed.

## Sources to avoid

- OSM-only reseeds: the withdrawn corpus used this approach; coordinates and map labels do not establish permission or classification.
- Aggregator/guide sites as the sole source, especially for current access, fees, closures and hazards.
- AI-generated lists or generated prose as evidence.
- Any source without a named responsible publisher.
